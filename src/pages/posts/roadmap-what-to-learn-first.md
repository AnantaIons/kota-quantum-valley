Quantum computing sits on **four pillars**: maths, physics, computer science and electronics. You don't need all of them on day one. You do need the maths first.

## The order that works

1. **Maths:** algebra, trigonometry, complex numbers, vectors and the dot product, matrices, eigenvalues, the tensor product, and probability. This is the language every later topic is written in.
2. **Physics:** the double-slit experiment, then modern physics: photons, matter waves, atomic structure and the basics of quantum mechanics.
3. **Physical chemistry:** quantum theory of atoms and orbitals. Useful for hardware and quantum chemistry.
4. **Computer science and Python:** logic gates, algorithms, and enough Python to run Qiskit.
5. **Electronics:** circuits, signals and measurement. This is the path into quantum hardware.
